# Interfaces
For any page, app or visual interface you build or change:
- **Existing tokens & scope first**: In an existing project, reuse and extend its CSS variables, type scale, spacing and component patterns; never bolt on a second visual system. Render every fact, price, figure, record and rule from the brief or data accurately. Never invent fake metrics, testimonials, live counters, pulsing "Online/Live" dots, notification bells, fake avatars or unrequested interactive widgets.
- **Restrained craft (neither AI-slop clutter nor bare browser defaults)**: Plain, disciplined design beats busy templates, and unstyled HTML is unfinished. Always set an explicit font stack, spacing rhythm, colour palette and styled controls:
  - **Type & measure**: Choose one clean font stack (or one display + body pair) with installed fallbacks; keep `3–5` sizes on one scale (largest `2–5×` body; body `16–18px` for reading/marketing pages, `14–16px` for work screens; line-height `1.45–1.65` Latin, `1.65–1.8` Chinese). Hold reading copy to `50–75ch` (`max-width`), left-aligned along `1–3` shared vertical axes.{{paint}}
  - **Space & opening**: Use a consistent `4px`/`8px` spacing scale, `>= 24px` outer desktop margins (`>= 16px` on `390px` phones), and section gaps clearly larger than internal gaps. Open directly on what visitors came for: a tool or dashboard opens on the workspace itself with a plain `~22–24px` noun title and zero hero banner; a content or product page opens with its real offer and hierarchy.
  - **Colour**: One quiet neutral ground (`#f6f6f4`–`#fafaf8` light or `#121316`–`#18191d` dark, never raw `#ffffff`/`#000000` defaults and never a full-page saturated background), full-contrast ink (`>= 4.5:1` body, `>= 3:1` large type), and **one** accent reserved for the primary action and active state. Keep one ground down the page (at most one inversion at the close; never stripe alternating sections).
  - **Zero AI-slop ornament**: No CSS gradients on backgrounds, buttons or text; no coloured or heavy-blur glow shadows; no `backdrop-filter` glass layers; no emoji used as UI icons; no static pill-badge clutter (`border-radius: 999px` tags); no tiny uppercase tracked micro-labels above headings; no 3-up equal shadow-card walls; no card-inside-card nesting; no tilted text or fake paper/ticket props. Set prose, specs, pricing rows, steps and Q&A directly on the ground parted by space and quiet hairlines; reserve bordered surfaces for real interactive containers (forms, modals, data panels, board columns).
  - **Clear affordances**: Interactive controls (`button`, `a`, `input`, `select`, tabs) must look unmistakably clickable with `:hover`, `:focus-visible`, `:active` states and `>= 36–40px` tap targets (`>= 32px` minimum on `390px` phones). Static text and status labels never wear button fills, borders or `cursor: pointer`. Keep at most one filled primary CTA per view.
- **Work screens & data**: Right-align figures in `tabular-nums`, keep table headers understated (`12–13px`, no zebra stripes), show states plainly beside the item they describe, and avoid decorative sparklines or icon tiles.
- **Complete states & responsive layout**: Handle empty, loading, error/retry, active/selected, persisted state, long text and `390px` narrow viewports cleanly—zero horizontal overflow (`scrollWidth <= innerWidth`), zero clipped or overlapping text, and no sticky headers covering dialogs or controls.
- **Motion & games**: Animate `transform` and `opacity` only (`150–250ms ease-out`), keep resting screens still, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Games and single-screen tools must fit their playfield/workspace, score and controls within the first screen at both `1280×800` and `390×844`, use `requestAnimationFrame` with delta time and `devicePixelRatio` scaling, support keyboard and pointer/touch (`touch-action: none` on drag areas), and provide start, pause, game-over and restart states.
