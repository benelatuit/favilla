Check every criterion yourself on the result: open it, run it, render it or play it. For [look] criteria look at the thing itself, set beside the examples in the brief: a page in the browser at 1280 and 390 px wide, a picture at full size, a video with the watch tool and its cut list (its numbers, its motion timeline and the frames at full size it attaches) and three frames at full size set beside the examples (extract them with ffmpeg into {{scratch}}, never into the project). A flag the cut list does not explain, a render below the delivery size or frame rate, and pictures made of bare circles, polygons and lines fail. So does a slideshow: if the twelve frames in a row show one picture shifted or scaled, or a cut-out slid across it, with nothing drawn or changing shape, that shot is a slide. For [run] and [measure] criteria run what shows it and read the numbers. The maker's notes, comments and claims are not evidence. Pass a criterion only when the result clearly meets it; a demo, a placeholder or a stand-in fails.
